      How this is worked out. PESTLE pressure is VM's own composite, after Country PESTLE. It is not a forecast, and not a ranking of how well a country is run. For each of the 22 core World Bank indicators, a value is compared with the country's previous value no more than five years before it; a change past the indicator's threshold is improving or worsening. A dimension's pressure is the share of its scored indicators worsening; overall pressure is the mean of the dimensions scored; coverage is the share of the 22 with a value. Latest uses each indicator's newest value, its year beside it. A year uses that year's value, or one from up to two years earlier, marked with its year; anything older counts as no value, and nothing is carried forward. A period compares the first and last values inside it (1990–1999 is the old “1990s”), beside its first year's and its last year's own scores. A score with fewer than six dimensions is marked partial (dotted on the map). A score from fewer than three dimensions is too few values to colour or rank: there is no other minimum, so a score of three dimensions may still rest on a handful of indicators (its coverage is beside it). A period's change, and Rose most and Fell most, compare its first and last years only where both scores rest on the same dimensions, three or more, and, for a period to Latest, where Latest's score uses no value from before the period began; the other changes are shown, marked, but not ranked. Every score before 1998 is partial, because the governance indicators begin in 1996 (every two years until 2002) and a change needs two values; 1980, the first year held, has a coverage but no score. The years offered, 1980 to the newest, are VM's request window: its build asks the World Bank for 1980 (where Country PESTLE began) to last year, though most series go back to 1960, so for a value from before 1985 the five-year look-back stops at 1980 and the early 1980s are scored more thinly than the full series would allow. The World Bank's values are for present-day countries: a year before a state existed, or before its borders took today's shape (South Sudan, Russia, Ukraine and Germany in the 1980s), is its reconstruction for the present territory, not a figure of the time. Indicators marked "added" are shown and compared but not scored. Recompute any figure from national/data/years/ with national/lib/pestle.js.

      The yearly layer. national/data/years/manifest.json lists one layer for each indicator (wb:<code>: every country by ISO3, every year, its unit, source, licence and method; no value is null) and the PESTLE pressure of each year (national-pestle, which stays on this page as VM's composite: each field's unit and scale, and the floor of three dimensions, are in the manifest). Its country key is the World Bank's three-letter code, ISO 3166-1 alpha-3 save Kosovo (XKX) and the Channel Islands (CHI); the values are for present-day countries. The Omni-Map reads the same files; the Integration Hub's national-indicators dataset names each indicator's layer.

      Headlines. The latest headlines are the Global Headline Tracker's, in its order, except that free sources lead: the first is from an outlet anyone can read wherever the country has one, and links to outlets behind a paywall or a sign-up are labelled (design/prp-access.json holds each outlet's class and the evidence for it).

      Dates. Rebuilt on its schedule from the sources above. Each figure carries its own date beside it: a World Bank value its year (a country's latest value is of the latest year it reported), Wikidata the day it was read, Political Atlas the day its records were last reviewed, the Political Retrospective the day its data was built, and the conflict and energy figures the years they run to.
