Sources. Battle deaths: UCDP Georeferenced Event Dataset v26.1 and candidate events (Sundberg & Melander 2013; Davies et al. 2026), CC BY 4.0, recorded event by event; for 1989–2008, where no monthly record exists, the UCDP Battle-Related Deaths Dataset v26.1 annual totals (Davies, Pettersson & Öberg 2026), CC BY 4.0 (ucdp.uu.se/downloads); for 1946–1988, annual totals from the PRIO Battle Deaths Dataset v3.1, whose page states no licence; kept until VM’s owner decides (Lacina & Gleditsch 2005). Annual totals are spread across months by UCDP events or recorded battles. Before 1946 no free source gives a war’s battle deaths, so none is shown. Each war's panel names the source it uses. Battles, sieges and operations: Wikidata (CC0). Battle figures: Wikidata (CC0); the casualties box of each battle’s English Wikipedia article, at the revision named in its window (Wikipedia, CC BY-SA 4.0: the figures are taken as facts, with at most a few of its words); since 1989, UCDP’s recorded clashes near a battle, shown apart. Borders: Cliopatria v0.2.1 (Bennett et al. 2025), CC BY 4.0, processed by Vitruvian Manager, one border a year; Cliopatria ends in 2024, so 2025 and 2026 are drawn on 2024’s borders. Small states Cliopatria has no row for: Natural Earth’s points (public domain). Each state is a Wikidata item (CC0), placed through the Retrospective Battlefield’s crosswalk. Articles, and the opening sentences the documentary reads from each war's English Wikipedia article, saying they are Wikipedia's: Wikipedia, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Method. States are coloured by the side they fought on that month and hatched while wholly occupied; a state takes its side from the day it entered the war (in World War II, as a rule, its declaration of war: Iran from 9 September 1943, Egypt from 24 February 1945) and leaves it on the day it left, and a state annexed before the war, as Austria was in March 1938, is on neither side, only held (Cliopatria draws it inside Germany, so the line under the map names it as held); in a civil war the contested state is shaded on its own row. Where Cliopatria draws a war state’s land inside another state’s row, that part is left uncoloured, and where it joins the land into the other state’s shape, the line under the map names it; a state drawn only as a far-off fragment is listed, not coloured. The borders are Cliopatria’s rows as it draws them, which need not match what is recognised in law: it draws Crimea in both Russia’s row and Ukraine’s from 2014 to 2023 and in Russia’s alone for 2024, so the map shows Crimea as Ukraine’s to December 2023 and as Russia’s from January 2024. Front lines are not drawn: no open data records them month by month. Figures marked ~ are estimates, not counts: a yearly or whole-war total shared out over its months (by UCDP's events where there are any, else by the battles under way in each month; a battle Wikidata dates only to its year weighs no month, as it could fall in any of them), so a month or a period is rounded to two significant figures, and a running or whole-war total that includes such months to three; UCDP's counts are shown as recorded, except that an event UCDP dates only to a period (a week, a month, a year) is shared out over the days of that period, so its deaths fall in the months it covers, not all on its first day; the month's figure says how many are such shares. A shared-out total adds up to its source's figure, and an annual total is spread only over the months of its own year. Battles are those Wikidata makes part of the war, reached only through items that fall within the war's dates and, below a wider operation that is also part of something outside the war, within the war's theatre (the area of its UCDP clashes and its other battles, with the territory of its states that reaches into it), so a wider or later campaign does not bring in another war's battles. Their names are Wikidata's English labels, except where a label was lately changed away from the title of the battle's English Wikipedia article, when VM keeps the earlier name, as the Omni-Map does (the killing of Yahya Sinwar, whose English label read ‘Shahadat of Yahya Sinwar’ from 24 to 27 November 2026). Their dates are Wikidata's at the precision it records them, leaving out any Wikidata marks as deprecated; a battle Wikidata dates only to the decade is listed as undated, counted in no month and not drawn on the map, and one with no date at all is left out; a battle with no end recorded, or whose end Wikidata gives before its start or after the war's end, is shown from its start with the reason, not drawn as a span; where Wikidata ends a battle more than a month after its English Wikipedia article does, it is drawn to the article's end and marked uncertain, with both dates; where VM has found that Wikidata's start for a battle, given with no reference, differs from its English Wikipedia article's, the battle is shown from the article's start, with both dates (the Battle of Iwo Jima: 19 February 1945, not 8 February); where Wikidata gives a battle several points in time, no start or end, and a name that does not say it lasted (a campaign, an offensive, a siege), each is a date of its own (a day, or only a month or a year where that is all Wikidata gives), not a span, and the battle is drawn and counted only in their months (the atomic bombings of Hiroshima and Nagasaki: 6 and 9 August 1945), and a name’s closing ‘in World War II’ says which war it was part of, not that it lasted (the bombing of Kobe: 4 February and 5 June 1945); and where it gives one date twice, at two precisions (a year, and a month in it), the finer is used. A battle’s figures: deaths are the killed, casualties the killed, wounded, missing and captured together, and neither stands in for the other; each figure says its kind (recorded, an estimate, or disputed or unreliable) and its source, and a battle with none says what was searched. They are never added into the war’s deaths by month. Over a period of months, every month between the handles is read: states take their side in its last month, a state on a side only earlier in the period is drawn faded with a broken outline in the side it last held, and a state is outlined where its side or occupation changed in any month of the period; the period's deaths are summed, its battles are those under way at some point in it, and UCDP clashes are grouped by place, sized by deaths a month.
