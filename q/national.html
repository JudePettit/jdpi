Sources. World Bank World Development Indicators and Worldwide Governance Indicators (CC BY 4.0) for the 41 indicators and the PESTLE scores; Wikidata (CC0) for systems of government, heads of state and capitals; Political Retrospective (Archigos and Wikidata) for leaders and elections since 1926; the UCDP/PRIO Armed Conflict Dataset (CC BY 4.0) and the Correlates of War project for conflict history; Realtime Battlefield (UCDP Candidate Events) for wars active this year; Kardashev Tracker for energy share; Historical Calendar for events since 1980; Global Headline Tracker for headlines. Country shapes: Natural Earth via world-atlas (public domain).

Method. PESTLE pressure follows Country PESTLE: each indicator's latest value is compared with the previous one, and a change past the indicator's threshold counts as improving or worsening. A dimension's pressure is the share of its scored indicators that are worsening; overall pressure is the mean of the six dimensions. The decade view compares the first and last values within each decade. Indicators marked "added" are shown and compared but not scored.
