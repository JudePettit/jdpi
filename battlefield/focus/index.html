Sources. Battle deaths: UCDP Georeferenced Event Dataset v26.1 and candidate events (Sundberg & Melander 2013; Davies et al. 2026), CC BY 4.0, recorded event by event; where no monthly record exists, PRIO Battle Deaths Dataset v3.1 annual totals (Lacina & Gleditsch 2005) or Correlates of War war totals (Sarkees & Wayman 2010), spread across months by UCDP events or recorded battles. Each war's panel names the source it uses. Battles, sieges and operations: Wikidata (CC0). Borders: CShapes 2.0 (Schvitz et al. 2022), CC BY-NC-SA. Articles: Wikipedia.

Method. States are coloured by the side they fought on that month and hatched while wholly occupied; in a civil war the contested state is shaded as a whole. Front lines are not drawn: no open data records them month by month. Months marked ~ are estimates; yearly and war totals are as published. Over a period of months, states take their side in its last month and are outlined where their side or occupation changed since its first; the period's deaths are summed, its battles are those under way at some point in it, and UCDP clashes are grouped by place, sized by deaths a month.
