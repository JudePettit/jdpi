Each position has ten active publishers. Each refresh uses the first ten in each position whose feeds return fresh stories, and reserves step in if one fails, so the positions stay equal. Positions follow AllSides Media Bias Ratings where available and Media Bias/Fact Check otherwise, adjusted to five steps. They describe editorial slant, not accuracy. ⚠ marks outlets those raters score as having mixed or low factual reliability: read them alongside other coverage.

Sources. RSS and Atom feeds from a catalogue of publishers balanced across five political positions, ten active per position with reserves; headlines link to the original articles.

Top stories. Headlines grouped as one story by Highbrowse’s own code, in your browser, from the four Omni News briefings’ stores, and counted by the Political Briefing’s publishers that ran them. A count of publishers, not a judgement of importance: the rule is under “How this is worked out”.

Free sources lead. In Top stories, a story’s first headline is from a publisher anyone can read wherever one ran it, a story only publishers behind a paywall or a sign-up ran never comes first, and a labelled headline has the same story’s free headline beside it (“Free at …”) where the grouping found one. In Latest, stories are newest first, except that the first story in the stream and in each position’s column is from a free publisher wherever the list has one; a story only publishers behind a paywall or a sign-up carry never comes first, and keeps its place lower down. Links to those publishers are labelled “Paywall” or “Sign-up”. Each publisher’s class, with the evidence and the day it was checked, is in design/prp-access.json; one that could not be checked counts as free.

Positions. AllSides Media Bias Ratings where available, otherwise Media Bias/Fact Check, adjusted to five steps. They describe editorial slant, not accuracy; ⚠ marks outlets that Media Bias/Fact Check rates mixed or low for factual reliability.

Refresh. Twice a day; stories are kept for seven days. Hard news only hides sport results, celebrity and entertainment coverage and lifestyle sections, but keeps stories about politics, law, courts, business or money in those fields.

Privacy. Your topics, your filter choice and the position you last picked are stored in this browser only.
