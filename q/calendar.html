Sources. Upcoming elections: IFES ElectionGuide and OSCE ODIHR listings, curated by hand, with saved snapshots in the PRP archive (Supabase). Past events: Political Retrospective (Archigos and Wikidata) for new leaders, coups, elections and referendums; the UCDP/PRIO Armed Conflict Dataset (CC BY 4.0) for outbreaks of armed conflict; Wikidata (CC0) for assassinations, battles, attacks, epidemics, disasters and treaties, and by class and date for referendums, summits and state visits, impeachments, confidence votes and budgets, supreme- and constitutional-court, ICJ and ICC rulings, protests and strikes, ceasefires and peace talks, sanctions, party leadership contests and congresses, inaugurations, and resignations (positions held that ended in resignation). Summits, past and scheduled: Wikipedia's lists of European Council meetings and of G7, G20, NATO, BRICS, SCO, APEC, ASEAN, Commonwealth and European Political Community summits, its COP and UN General Assembly articles, and its national electoral calendars for scheduled referendums. The news since January 2026: Wikipedia's Current events portal (CC BY-SA) for inaugurations, resignations, parliamentary votes, court rulings, protests and strikes, ceasefires and peace talks, sanctions, party contests, summits and visits, each with the news report it cites; one protest wave or round of talks is shown at its main moments rather than every day. Full accounts in the pop-out: Wikipedia (CC BY-SA).
