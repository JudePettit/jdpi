Month by Month

Each month, for every war VM follows, and every country once UCDP's per-country figures are taken in: did UCDP record more deaths, fewer, or no large change against the three months before? Every mark is Vitruvian Manager's own calculation from figures it publishes, with what changed, the period, the evidence and its confidence beside it. Where the figures are too thin to say, there is no mark, and the page says why.

How this is worked out

Every mark here is Vitruvian Manager's own calculation from figures it publishes. It is not an analyst's judgement, and nothing here is a forecast.

Fighting

For each war and each country, VM takes UCDP's recorded deaths (its best estimate) and its number of events in one month, and compares them with the three months before. When UCDP dates an event only to a span of days, its deaths are shared out over those days and it counts as an event in each month it touches. A month is marked "More deaths recorded" when its deaths are at least one and a half times the median of the three months before (the middle one of the three), at least 25 more, and above each of the three; "Fewer deaths recorded" when they are at most two thirds of it, at least 25 fewer, and below each of the three; otherwise "No large change by this rule". Events are compared with their median the same way (at least 5 more or fewer), as a check. A "No large change" mark names the condition the month did not meet. Because the rule uses proportions, a change of hundreds of deaths in a large war can fall below it. The rule does not adjust for fighting seasons: each mark shows the same month a year earlier where VM holds it.

What is counted

Realtime Battlefield's wars and the countries count all organised violence UCDP records (fighting between states and armed groups, between armed groups, and against civilians); War Focus's wars count fighting involving a state only. Until Realtime Battlefield's file is rebuilt with VM's shared count, its wars count each event in the month it starts, as its page does. A war both cover is listed once in the bulletin, by War Focus.

No mark

There is none when UCDP has not yet published the month (it does so three to four weeks after the month ends); when a war has ended before the month; when a month of the four is not a whole month in the figures (War Focus counts a war only from the day it starts and to the day it ends, so a first or last month can hold a few days); when a month of the four is an estimate or comes from another UCDP dataset; when there are fewer than 10 events in the month or an average under 10 a month before it, or under 25 deaths in the four months; when one event decides it (the mark would not hold with the month's largest event taken out; for fewer, nor with each of the four months' own largest event taken out of it, nor with the largest event of the three months before taken out of its month, a test left out when every one of the four months is led by one event or report holding more than half its deaths, since only the like-for-like test then compares like with like). Some of UCDP's "events" are reports covering a whole month, such as a monthly tally of losses: a mark calls them reports and gives the days they cover; when, for fewer, more than a quarter of the deaths in the three months before come from events UCDP dates only to a span running across months and a quarter or less of this month's do, since a later release may yet add such deaths to it; for a country, when the four months span two years and a UCDP conflict is counted in only one (UCDP counts a conflict only in a year with 25 or more battle deaths), or begin before its independence or unification (or, for Russia and Serbia, before the Soviet Union and Yugoslavia split, since UCDP counts the whole state under their codes until then); and when three of the checks below apply. The page says which.

Confidence

A mark starts at high and drops one step for each of these: fewer than 25 events in the month, or an average under 25 before it; one event holds more than half the month's deaths; more than a quarter of any of the four months' deaths come from events UCDP dates only to a span running across months (or the figures cannot tell); deaths and events point opposite ways; a month of the four comes from UCDP's candidate events, which are provisional (UCDP revises them, and its final figures come the next year). One step down is medium, two is low, three or more means no mark. Every mark and square from candidate events also says "provisional".

Volatility

For each country, the average of its daily volatility scores in a month is compared with the average over the three months before: 4 points or more higher is "More volatile", 4 or more lower "Less volatile". There is no volatility mark before April 2027 (every day of the four months must have been scored against a full 90 days, by one method) or while most days had little news coverage, since a rise in coverage alone can move a score. The 4 points are a starting value, to be checked against the data in April 2027. Volatility is never added to deaths and never changes a deaths mark: it is drawn from news reports.

Calendar events are shown beside a month but never change its mark. The watch list is a list of dated events from VM's calendars, and of countries whose volatility rose over the last week: dated things to watch, not predictions.
