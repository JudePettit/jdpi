Sources. Events: UCDP Candidate Events Dataset (Hegre et al. 2020), CC BY 4.0, released monthly by the Uppsala Conflict Data Program. News: front pages of the outlets tracked by the Global Headline Tracker over the last 30 days. Basemap: Esri Light Gray and Dark Gray Canvas (Esri, HERE, Garmin, © OpenStreetMap contributors), under the Esri Master License Agreement, which is not a free licence: kept until VM’s owner decides. Countries: Natural Earth 1:50m (public domain), through world-atlas 2.0.2 (ISC); Tuvalu, which it does not draw, is a dot.

Method. Events are grouped into wars and warzones, and each month is summarised in plain English; deaths are UCDP's best estimates. Armed violence outside the named wars is grouped by country; where a country’s comes to under 25 deaths in the year, it is kept in one group, “Other recorded armed violence (under 25 deaths in a country this year)”. A country’s figures count the events UCDP files under that country. A country’s and a war’s figures here count each event in the month it starts; Month by Month shares out an event UCDP dates only to a span of days over those days, so its figures for a month can differ. Candidate events are preliminary and can change when UCDP publishes its final annual data. VM checks for a new UCDP release weekly, and an open page reloads its data every six hours.
