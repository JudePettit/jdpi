      Sources. Every fact on this page (people, life dates, nationalities, fields, institutions, places and coordinates) comes from Wikidata, which is public domain (CC0), fetched through its API and refreshed monthly. Articles: Wikipedia. World outline: Natural Earth (public domain), via world-atlas.

      Selection. The choice of breakthroughs, their dates, titles and one-line notes are PRP’s own, informed by the standard histories of science. No copyrighted reference work, list or text is reproduced.

      Method. Each breakthrough is placed by the first rule that finds a location: a place set by hand where Wikidata would mislead; Wikidata’s location of discovery; the site itself; the discoverer’s employer, affiliation or university in the year of the breakthrough; their main institution; their work location; where they studied; their birthplace; and finally an approximate place set by hand. The rule used is shown on every card. Dates before about 500 BC are approximate.
