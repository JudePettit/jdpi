    Tier 1 are the flagship journals (Nature, Science, Cell, PNAS, Physical Review Letters); tier 2 other major journals; tier 3 quality science journalism and agency newsrooms. “Major journals first” orders each day by tier. A feed with no default discipline is multidisciplinary: its items are sorted into disciplines by keywords. “In feed” is what the feed returned at the last check, after corrections, errata and sponsored posts were dropped.

    Sources. RSS and Atom feeds from the journals and science desks listed under Sources: Springer Nature, AAAS, Cell Press, PNAS, the American Physical Society, Wiley (Angewandte Chemie, AGU), IOP Publishing and the AAS, PLOS, the Royal Society of Chemistry’s Chemistry World, ACS’s C&EN, NASA, ESA and others. Headlines, links and short summaries only; every item links to its publisher, and the text remains theirs. DOIs resolve through doi.org.

    Method. Items are classed by their feed’s default discipline, refined by a keyword classifier (an item can belong to several disciplines; multidisciplinary feeds are classed by keywords alone, and items matching none appear under All). Papers are journal research articles, letters, reports and reviews; journals’ own news, comment and News & Views count as News. Corrections, errata and sponsored posts are left out. Duplicates (same URL or DOI, or near-identical titles across outlets) are merged and the copy from the highest tier is kept. Items stay for seven days, at most 2,500 in all and 150 per feed. Times are shown in your time zone; feeds that give only a date are grouped by that date.

    Refresh. Every six hours by a GitHub Actions workflow (science/scripts/build-briefing.mjs).
