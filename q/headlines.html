Top stories. For each covered country, the top three front-page stories of its five most-read news outlets, collected once a day into the PRP headline archive and translated into English; links go to the original-language articles. A day lists each outlet’s stories in front-page order; a week, month or year ranks the stories that stayed on front pages longest.

Fallback. When the archive cannot be reached, the tracker queries GDELT DOC 2.0 ↗: the 100 highest relevance-ranked results, from which up to 20 stories are spread across publishers. Relevance is a proxy for prominence, not a popularity score, and English-language coverage varies by country. Windows you load are also saved in this browser.
