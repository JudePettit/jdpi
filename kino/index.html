        Sources. Each documentary is written by PRP in its own words from the scholarly books and articles it lists, with names, dates and images checked against Wikidata (CC0). Maps: world-atlas 2.0.2 (Natural Earth, public domain), drawn with d3 7.9.0 on present-day borders for orientation. Images: Wikimedia Commons, each credited on screen with its author and licence. Music: public-domain and CC0 recordings from Wikimedia Commons. Voice: your browser's own speech synthesis (a British English voice where one is installed).

        Method. Figures for deaths are given as ranges from named sources, never as single uncertain numbers. Arrows and front lines are simplified sketches of movements described in the sources, marked approximate where they are. A full stop with no space after it is read as “point” between digits and “dot” between letters, never as the end of a sentence.
